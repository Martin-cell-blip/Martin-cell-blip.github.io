决策系统档案The Decision Ledger

预测 ≠ 决策Prediction ≠ Decision

预测 + 目标 + 约束 + 取舍 + 责任Prediction + Objective + Constraints + Trade-offs + Accountability

模型算得出下月销量下滑。这句话里没有任何一个动作。可选的动作有四个：降价、减库存、加投广告、退出品类。选哪一个，取决于这个品类要的是收入还是利润，能担多大风险，预算和库存还剩多少空间。放弃另外三条路要付出什么，同样要算进去。目标和权限一旦写清楚，AI 可以排序、推荐，也可以直接执行。它定不了这家公司把什么放在第一位，也不为最终结果负责。 A model can tell you sales will fall next month. There is no action anywhere in that sentence. Four actions are open: cut the price, cut stock, spend more on ads, exit the category. Which one wins depends on whether this category is run for revenue or for margin, how much risk it can carry, and how much budget and inventory are left. The cost of giving up the other three counts too. Once objectives and authority are set down, AI can rank, recommend and even execute. It cannot settle what this company puts first, and it does not answer for the result.

五件代表作落在五类业务——电商选品与直播 GTM、数据与 Agent 基础设施、结算与经营决策、消费健康产品、品类经营与促销。数据、规则与决策目标各不相同，做法是同一套：先弄清谁在做决策、哪些指标真正影响结果，再决定用什么数据、什么模型、哪一步交给人确认。 Five flagship builds across five businesses — e-commerce product discovery and livestream GTM, data and agent infrastructure, settlement and operating decisions, a consumer health product, category and promotion management. Data, rules and decision targets differ; the method does not: work out who decides and which metrics actually move the outcome, then choose the data, the model, and the step a human must confirm.

DecisionOps AI · 人工确认的决策自动化Human-confirmed Decision Automation

双业务 Agent——结算风控与商家经营。规则引擎负责金额与判定，大模型只负责循证解释：每条建议必须带证据引用，引用 ID 必须在证据集内；样本不足 30 单或 500 浏览时拒绝出建议。除正常付款外的所有动作都进人工审批，系统不执行任何资金动作。200 例固定种子评测与 9 项测试接入 CI，字段来源全披露。 Two business agents — settlement risk and merchant operations. The rules engine owns amounts and verdicts; the LLM only explains with evidence: every recommendation must cite evidence IDs that exist in the evidence set, and under 30 orders or 500 views it declines to advise. Every action except a routine payment goes to human approval, and the system never moves money. 200 fixed-seed eval cases and 9 tests run in CI, with full field provenance.

问题Problem
看板不缺，缺的是「下一步动哪个 SKU、凭什么」；拒付或追回之前必须拿得出凭据。Dashboards are plentiful; what is missing is "which SKU to act on and on what grounds" — you need evidence before you refuse or claw back a payment.
做了什么Built

基于 10 万+ 条公开电商交易记录（112,650 条订单明细）的经营决策闭环：品类结构 → 选品汰换 → 控价情景 → 大促复盘（估算 ROI），四模块共用一套 GMV 口径，跨模块对账容差为 0。诊断出高价带以 25.8% 的 SKU 贡献 62.6% GMV 的结构倒挂；输出 374 个低效复核 / 汰换候选 SKU，静态情景下移除 15.6% 货盘对应约 9.6% 的历史 GMV。 An operating-decision loop over 100k+ public e-commerce transaction records (112,650 order lines): structure → assortment → pricing scenarios → promotion review (estimated ROI), four modules on one GMV definition, cross-module reconciliation at zero tolerance. Surfaced a skew where 25.8% of SKUs drive 62.6% of GMV; flagged 374 low-efficiency review / delisting candidates — in a static scenario, removing 15.6% of the range corresponds to about 9.6% of historical GMV.

AI 应用治理：我按这份清单构建AI governance: the checklist I build to

六条原则。每条标明已经在哪个项目里落地；没有落地记录的，是我设计时的清单项，不是已交付的功能。Six principles. Each names the project where it is implemented; items without an implementation record are checklist items I design to, not shipped features.

01 · 最小权限Least privilege

模型只拿只读权限和决策所需的字段；写入、付款、发布一律不在模型手里。The model gets read-only access and only the fields a decision needs; writes, payments and publishing never sit with the model.

  • DecisionOps：大模型无写权限，不执行资金动作DecisionOps: the LLM has no write access and never moves money
  • Agentic Data Platform：SELECT-only SQL guardAgentic Data Platform: SELECT-only SQL guard
02 · 密钥与凭证API keys & credentials

密钥只在运行环境，不进仓库、不进前端；没有密钥时系统退回规则路径，而不是报错或伪造输出。Keys live in the runtime environment, never in the repo or the client; without a key the system falls back to the rules path instead of failing or faking output.

  • 结算复核 Copilot：未配置密钥即停用模型，退回规则理由Settlement copilot: with no key configured the model is disabled and the rule rationale stands
03 · OAuth 与授权范围OAuth & scopes

接平台 API 时只申请最窄的 scope，刷新令牌留在服务端，授权可撤销、可审计。When connecting a platform API, request the narrowest scope, keep refresh tokens server-side, make grants revocable and auditable.

  • 清单项——尚无项目接入 OAuth 平台Checklist item — no project connects to an OAuth platform yet
04 · 操作日志与证据留痕Logs & audit trail

每次运行有清单，每个决定有人、有时间、有依据；证据包可哈希校验，经办与复核分离。Every run has a manifest; every decision has an owner, a timestamp and grounds; evidence packs are hash-checkable; preparer and reviewer are separate people.

  • 结算复核 Copilot：运行清单与证据包记 SHA-256；人工决定为 SQLite 事务，冲突 409Settlement copilot: SHA-256 on manifests and evidence packs; human decisions as SQLite transactions, 409 on conflict
05 · 人工确认Human confirmation

AI 排序、推荐、起草；凡是动钱、动量、动价的动作都经人确认，样本不足就拒答。AI ranks, recommends and drafts; anything that moves money, volume or price is confirmed by a person, and on thin data the system declines.

  • DecisionOps：除正常付款外全部进审批；不足 30 单 / 500 浏览拒答DecisionOps: everything but a routine payment goes to approval; refuses under 30 orders / 500 views
  • 结算复核：13,324 条转人工，自动执行 0 条Settlement copilot: 13,324 rows to humans, 0 auto-executed
06 · 提示词注入与数据泄露Prompt injection & data leakage

输出受合同约束——结构、引用、拒答；评测集里放注入条目；给模型的只有决策需要的证据字段。Outputs are contract-bound — structure, citations, refusal; an injection case sits in the eval set; the model only sees the evidence fields a decision needs.

  • 再做对：评测集含一条注入条目，合同违规即拒绝并重试redo-right: the eval set includes one injection item; contract violations are refused and retried GitHub ↗
  • DecisionOps：引用 ID 必须全在证据集内，否则回退模板DecisionOps: citation IDs must all exist in the evidence set, or the output falls back to a template
