决策系统档案The Decision Ledger

预测 ≠ 决策Prediction ≠ Decision

预测 + 目标 + 约束 + 取舍 + 责任Prediction + Objective + Constraints + Trade-offs + Accountability

模型算得出下月销量下滑。这句话里没有任何一个动作。可选的动作有四个：降价、减库存、加投广告、退出品类。选哪一个，取决于这个品类要的是收入还是利润，能担多大风险，预算和库存还剩多少空间。放弃另外三条路要付出什么，同样要算进去。目标和权限一旦写清楚，AI 可以排序、推荐，也可以直接执行。它定不了这家公司把什么放在第一位，也不为最终结果负责。 A model can tell you sales will fall next month. There is no action anywhere in that sentence. Four actions are open: cut the price, cut stock, spend more on ads, exit the category. Which one wins depends on whether this category is run for revenue or for margin, how much risk it can carry, and how much budget and inventory are left. The cost of giving up the other three counts too. Once objectives and authority are set down, AI can rank, recommend and even execute. It cannot settle what this company puts first, and it does not answer for the result.

四件代表作落在四类业务——消费影像、平台结算风控、采销经营、家居日用，数据、规则与决策目标各不相同。进入陌生领域，我先弄清谁在做决策、哪些指标真正影响结果，再决定用什么数据和方法。 Four flagship builds across four businesses — consumer imaging, platform settlement risk, category buying and home goods — each with its own data, rules and decision targets. Entering an unfamiliar one, I work out who decides and which metrics actually move the outcome before picking data or method.

DJI Mimo「AI 一键成片」· 产品需求文档Product Requirements

从自采 8,223 条 App Store 评论与 706 条 B 站评论定位痛点，到把「什么算剪砸了」写成 84 条可机械判定的验收：G1 能不能发布 / G2 设备遥测是否真被用上 / G3 你会不会不改一帧直接发出去，三级全过才算通过。MVP 只留「运动燃片」一个场景。首轮评测尚未执行——文档给的是判据，不是战报。 From 8,223 self-collected App Store reviews and 706 Bilibili comments to a written-down definition of failure: 84 mechanically checkable cases across three gates — G1 shippable / G2 is the device telemetry actually used / G3 would you post it without changing a frame. All three must pass. The MVP keeps exactly one scenario. The first eval round has not been run — this is the protocol, not results.

用户在大疆拍、却把素材导去剪映剪——工作流断在出片这步，而这块能力目前由第三方承担。大疆手里有云台电机指令、IMU、逐帧主体轨迹和 GPS 速度曲线，这些是纯视觉推断不出来的拍摄现场真值。所以 AI 只放在感知与判断环节（分辨运镜与手抖、定位速度峰值），核心剪辑仍是确定性流水线，大模型只碰文案层。「什么算剪砸了」先写成 84 条可机械判定的验收。首轮评测尚未执行——这里给的是判据，不是战报。Users shoot on DJI and edit in CapCut: the workflow breaks at export, and that capability is currently outsourced. DecisionOps AI · 决策运营台Decision Ledger

双业务 Agent——结算风控与商家经营。规则引擎负责计算，大模型负责循证解释：每条建议必须携带证据引用；样本不足 30 单或 500 浏览时主动拒绝出建议，转人工审批。200 例固定种子评测接入 CI，字段来源全披露。 Two business agents — settlement risk and merchant operations. The rules engine does the math; the LLM explains with evidence: every recommendation must carry citations; under 30 orders or 500 views it refuses to advise at all and hands off to human review. 200 fixed-seed eval cases wired into CI; full field-provenance disclosure.

看板不缺，缺的是「下一步动哪个 SKU、凭什么」。财务运营真正的痛点不是发现异常，而是解释异常——拒付或追回之前必须拿得出凭据。所以金额与判定交给规则引擎，必须可复算；大模型只做规则写不出来的那件事：把证据串成人能读的理由，并在样本不足时说清为什么不给建议。它没有写权限。合成数据演示，无真实用户与业务收益。Dashboards are not scarce; knowing which SKU to act on and on what grounds is. The real pain is not spotting an exception but explaining it — you need evidence before you refuse or claw back a payment. So amounts and verdicts stay in the rules engine, recomputable; the LLM does only what rules cannot: turn evidence into a readable rationale, and say why it declines to advise on thin data. It has no write access. Synthetic-data demo — no real users or business outcomes.

品类采销经营模拟盘Category Management Simulator

基于 10 万+ 真实订单的采销决策闭环：品类结构 → 选品汰换 → 控价情景 → 大促复盘，四模块共用一套 GMV 口径。诊断出高价带以 25.8% SKU 贡献 62.6% GMV 的结构倒挂。 A buying-and-merchandising loop over 100k+ real orders: structure → assortment → pricing → promotion review, four modules on one GMV definition. Surfaced a skew where 25.8% of SKUs drive 62.6% of GMV.

品类结构、选品汰换、控价、大促复盘，在多数团队里是四张彼此对不上的表——口径不统一，四个结论就无法互证。这里刻意不用大模型：GMV、毛利与汰换影响都是确定性计算，交给模型只会让结果不可复算，判断力该花在定义口径和设约束上。四模块共用一套 GMV 口径，跨模块对账做成管线硬断言，校验失败即中止。数据为公开的 2016–2018 巴西 Olist 平台集，无成本与流量字段，结论只到方法论层。Structure, assortment, pricing and promotion review usually live in four spreadsheets that disagree — without one shared definition they cannot corroborate each other. There is deliberately no LLM here: GMV, margin and delisting impact are deterministic computations, and a model would only make them non-reproducible. Judgement belongs in defining the metric and setting constraints. Four modules share one GMV definition, with reconciliation as a hard pipeline assertion that halts on failure. Public 2016–2018 Brazilian Olist data, without cost or traffic fields; conclusions hold at the method level only.

家居日用品类行业研究Home & Daily Goods Industry Study

平台战略视角的七章行研：三锚点交叉测算品类约 2000 亿、可获得盘 240–300 亿；识别并校正咨询机构口径污染；结论——份额行业而非增长行业，大类分散但细分单品可集中。 A seven-chapter study from a platform-strategy lens: the category triangulated at ≈¥200bn with a ¥24–30bn obtainable pool; corrected a consulting-firm definition contamination; verdict — a share game, not a growth game, with concentratable niches.

进一个陌生品类，先要回答的不是「多大」而是「这是抢份额还是等增长」——两者对应完全不同的打法。而现成报告的口径互相污染，直接引用等于把结论建在别人的定义上，所以规模测算全程不用模型：三个独立口径各算一遍再交叉，是为了让每一步都能被别人重算。AI 只用在检索与初筛，数字逐条人工核对并编号。60+ 条编号来源；6 组对不上的数字单列一章，其中 2 组至今未解决。Entering an unfamiliar category, the first question is not how big it is but whether it is a share game or a growth game — the two imply opposite strategies. Published reports contaminate each other's definitions, so citing them builds your conclusion on someone else's terms; the sizing therefore uses no model at all: three independent anchors computed separately and cross-checked, so every step can be recomputed. AI is used only for retrieval and screening. 60+ numbered sources; six conflicting figures are listed in their own chapter, two still unresolved.
